Reason

 

I’ve been thinking about why I didn’t celebrate your birthday the way I should have, and I want to be real with you. Growing up, I never really got to celebrate mine — it was treated like just another day. Over time, I stopped celebrating a lot of things, and I carried that mindset with me. But I realize now that I shouldn’t assume everyone was raised the same way. Sometimes I feel like I’m not “normal” when it comes to celebrating, because for me those days often carried regret. So I pulled away from them. But now I understand birthdays are meant to be joyful, and yours especially deserves that. Even though the day has passed, I’d still love to do something with you — no matter how small or simple — just to celebrate you in a way that makes you happy. You mean a lot to me, and I want to make sure you feel that.
